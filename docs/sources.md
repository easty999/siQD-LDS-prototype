# where this comes from

checked on 17 october 2026. these papers help explain the plan, but their results don't tell us what this particular prototype will do.

1. [lopez-delgado et al., cdse/cds lds, scientific reports 7, 14104 (2017)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-14269-0). the full article was checked. corrects the original citation; reports jsc 32.5 to 37.0 mA/cm2 and uses solar-simulator i-v, eqe and reflectance measurements. antireflection contributes. relevant as a measurement precedent, not a recipe to copy or validation of the current model.
2. [lopez-delgado et al., si-qd lds, microsystem technologies 24, 495–502](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00542-017-3405-x). the publisher's abstract was accessible; the detailed method needs subscription access. establishes direct si-qd prior art; reported emission is 450–650 nm, different from the model. do not infer a complete synthesis procedure from the abstract.
3. [si-qd/pmma films, acs applied materials & interfaces, doi 10.1021/acsami.9b22903](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.9b22903). the publisher's search abstract was available, but the full page returned a 403 error. demonstrates doctor-bladed si-qd/pmma on glass and identifies aggregation/scattering as a problem. supports a removable film route; does not establish this package's concentrations or drying conditions.
4. [si-qd concentrators and downshifters, acs photonics, doi 10.1021/acsphotonics.1c00550](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsphotonics.1c00550). the publisher's abstract was checked. its different perovskite/lsc geometry shows that uv response improvement need not yield broadband pce improvement. do not transfer its device performance to a silicon cell.
5. [nlr/nrel reference am1.5 spectra](https://www.nlr.gov/grid/solar-resource/spectra-am1.5). official provenance and units for astm g173. local workbook headers determine the column selection. see the linked data disclaimer before redistributing the derived csv publicly.
6. [nrel photovoltaic calibrations, report 66873](https://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy17osti/66873.pdf). official calibration guidance for spectral response, reference devices, spectral mismatch and temperature control. supports the measurement requirements here; this project is not a certified calibration service.
7. [sinbiosys si-alkyl supplier page](https://www.sinbiosys.com/product/si-alkyl/). primary vendor listing: si-rv, 2 mL, 200 micromolar silicon-core concentration, 750–850 nm emission, toluene dispersion, listed eur450 excluding vat. vendor states lot absorption/emission/qy characterization. price and availability require a quote; core molarity is not particle molarity or a mass concentration of ligand-coated dots.

before making the films, get the full method and the safety and compatibility details for the actual lot. if that access isn't available, try to get a finished, characterized film from a lab.
