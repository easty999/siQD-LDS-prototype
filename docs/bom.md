# what to get

the plan needs one reusable holder and at least three separately made samples of each film condition. default slide envelope is 75 x 25 x 1 mm; measure actual parts before printing. most costs here are rough planning amounts, not quotes. the dated supplier listing is marked separately. borrow the lab instruments if you can.

| item | quantity / specification | procurement and purpose |
|---|---|---|
| characterized silicon nanocrystal dispersion or finished si-qd films | one 2 mL pilot lot; target red/nir 700–850 nm emission, certificate and sds | candidate sinbiosys si-rv: 2 mL, 200 uM silicon-core basis, toluene; eur450 ex vat listed 17 oct 2026. obtain quote/shipping/import terms. prefer collaborating lab's characterized films if available. no purchase made. |
| pmma host | 1–5 g, optical-grade, documented molecular weight | lab stock. confirm dissolution and ligand compatibility with vendor; polymer-only control uses same batch. |
| compatible solvent | approximately 20–50 mL, lab-grade | for the candidate toluene dispersion, use lab-approved matching solvent and waste route. do not mix aqueous and hydrophobic formulations by assumption. |
| plain optical slides | 20, measured 75 x 25 mm, same lot | 3 uncoated; 3 host; 3 each low/high qd; remaining pilot/spares. confirm transmission at excitation wavelength; use fused silica if glass blocks the required uv. |
| capped glass vials and pipettes | 6–10 vials; 20–200 and 100–1000 uL pipettes with suitable tips | solvent-compatible tools, mixer, microbalance access; no dispensing by guessed drops. |
| coating applicator | 1 adjustable doctor blade; 100 um wet-gap starting proposal | alternatively calibrated shims and glass drawdown edge in a hood. the wet gap doesn't tell you the final dry thickness. |
| si cell and spare | 2 single-junction cells with contacts outside a 20 x 20 mm illuminated region | prefer pretabbed cell, documented front stack and eqe. a packaged mini-module may block uv and conceal cell area; characterize its window before use. |
| reference detector | 1 calibrated si reference cell; or stable second cell for relative drift tracking only | coplanar with test cell, never covered by changing test coupons. |
| smu / i-v tracer | 1 borrowed unit with current range covering the cell | four-wire where possible; low burden for isc. dmm-only route supports screening, not full efficiency. |
| multimeters / temperature sensors | 2 meters and 2 contact sensors | monitor reference current, test/reference temperature and dark offsets. check resolution versus expected differences. |
| light source | borrow am1.5g simulator with calibration record | white lamp is only source-specific screening; enclosed 365/405 nm led optional for excitation studies, with measured spectrum. |
| optical instruments | uv-visible transmission/reflectance, pl spectrometer; integrating sphere for film plqy | book shared facility. collimated transmission alone conflates scattering and absorption. |
| mounting kit | 1 tray print, 4 spacers, 2 thin aperture masks, insulating board, 2 edge clips, soft pads | cad included. use measured opaque nonluminescent masks; cell mounting stays independent of slide swaps. |
| safety / storage | hood, lab ppe per sds, uv enclosure/interlock, amber storage, labeled solvent/nanoparticle waste | managed by supervising lab; do not equate silicon composition with safe loose powder or solvent. |

local nonchemical mechanical/electrical consumables allowance: inr2,000–6,000 if meters, cell and printing are already accessible; additional instruments, cell purchase, chemistry, shipping and facility fees are excluded. this is an unquoted planning envelope, not a total project price. the biggest questions are getting the quantum dots and access to the instruments. a collaborator supplying three matched finished coupons per condition can remove the need to buy chemistry.

before committing money, obtain: lot identity, absorption/emission spectra, plqy method and uncertainty, concentration basis, ligands, solvent, sds, storage lifetime, recommended host and drying conditions. require adequate emission and host transparency; a generic silicon powder or a “quantum dot” dye does not meet the project definition. a cheaper equivalent supplier hasn't been verified yet.
